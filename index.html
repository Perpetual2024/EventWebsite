About the Event

Welcome to MKU Freshers Night! This exciting event is designed to help new students settle into campus life, meet new friends, and feel part of the vibrant Mount Kenya University community. It is a special evening filled with music, performances, laughter, and unforgettable memories as students come together to celebrate the beginning of a new academic journey. Whether you are meeting classmates for the first time or simply looking for a fun and welcoming place to be, this event offers the perfect opportunity to connect, enjoy great entertainment, and create lasting experiences. Come with your friends, enjoy the atmosphere, and make the most of this exciting night.
