WELCOME TO CAMPUS

About the event

MKU Freshers Night is designed to help new students settle into campus life, meet new friends, and feel part of the Mount Kenya University community.

Enjoy an evening of music, performances, and laughter as students come together to celebrate the beginning of a new academic journey. Whether you are meeting classmates for the first time or catching up with friends, the night is a chance to connect and create lasting memories.
